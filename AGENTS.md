# AGENTS.md — Colophon

LLM-driven publishing, editorial, and layout suite for ComfyUI.

## Brand Identity & Standards
- **Product Name**: **Colophon** (Repo: `colophon`)
- **Accent**: Oxblood / Ink Purple (`#7747B8` / Dark: `#9A72D6`)
- **Job Line**: *"Manuscript in, typeset book out. Automated literary editing, art direction, and layout composition."*
- **Symbol**: 24×24u, 1.75u stroke, open publication spread (3 rules + framing box) with 1 solid spine binding pip.
- **Suite Context**: Form & Noise Atelier Loose Endorsed Family. Space Grotesk (Wordmarks), Inter (UI), IBM Plex Mono (Code).

## Core Nodes & Architecture
- **Literary Editor**: LLM pagination by dramatic beats.
- **Editorial Art Director**: Latent style & character embedding locking across scenes.
- **Layout Compositor**: Renders formatted book pages with real typography and PDF export.
